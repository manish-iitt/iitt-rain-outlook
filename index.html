How to read this · limits
  • Classes (per 3 hours): dry under 0.2 mm, light 0.2–2.5, moderate 2.5–7.5, heavy 7.5–20, very heavy above 20 mm.
  • Why 9 km? On 20–21 Oct the 9 km run produced rain in the same periods people reported it; the finer 3 km and 1 km runs gave almost none. This is one event, so it is not proof the 9 km run is generally better.
  • Amounts are uncertain. In earlier events the 9 km run gave several times more rain than IMD measured. The 3 km and 1 km runs forecast far less for 22 Oct (about 7 mm for the day against 17 mm here). Verification against IMD data is under way.
  • The forecast is one model run from one set of starting data, and it gets less reliable the further ahead you look.
  • Coastline is approximate. Map covers 9.5–17.5°N, 75.5–83.5°E.
